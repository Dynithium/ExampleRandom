# Elderville - Village Prototype

A pixel art village game where you explore Elderville, fight scrap bots, and survive in your life suit.

## Features
- Walk around a village with houses
- Talk to NPCs
- Enter houses (PRESS ENTER when in front of door)

## Controls
- **Arrow Keys** or **WASD**: Move
- **SPACE**: Talk to people
- **ENTER**: Enter/exit houses

## Run
Open `index.html` in your browser.
