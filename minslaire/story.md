# Story

## The Village of Elderville

A small, humble village tucked between the western hills and the eastern forest. Elderville has no machines, no technology—just wooden homes, dirt paths, and the quiet flow of the river beyond the treeline. The villagers live simply, trading crops and stories, knowing that their very survival depends on the strange suits they wear.

The village is led by a **Council of Elders**, who gather in the **Blue House** at the center of town. The council settles disputes, keeps the old knowledge, and interprets the quiet signals of the life suits. Their word is law, but their wisdom is what has kept Elderville alive this long.

## The Life Suits

Every villager is born into a **life suit**—a woven bio-synthetic membrane engineered by humans after World War 3 that bonds to the skin at birth. It breathes, filters, and sustains them. **If you take it off, you die.** The suit and the wearer are one.

## The Coming of the Scrap

Times changed when twisted machines—rusted relics of a forgotten age—began crawling out of the forest. The villagers call them **Scrap Bots**: broken drones, rusted walkers, and heavy mechs that patrol the woods with glowing crimson eyes. 

The dark truth, however, is a plot twist: the elders of the village actually turned the Scrap Bots on themselves. They knew that activating the machines would cause the hidden location of the Elemental Box to reveal itself through a blazing blue light beacon.

## Minslaire

You are **Minslaire**, a young villager who lives in a small **red house** just west of the Blue House. Your parents are gone—lost to the machines years ago—and the blade they left behind is all you have of them. Your life suit hums quietly against your skin, a constant reminder that you carry your own survival with you wherever you go.

You live with your younger brother **Tinslaire**, a bright-eyed boy who looks up to you. He's too young to fight, but old enough to understand the danger. Every morning he asks the same question: "Will the machines come today?"

You never know what to say.

## The Elemental Box

The elders have called an emergency meeting. Something has been discovered deep in the eastern forest—an artifact from the old world, before the suits, before the scrap. They call it the **Elemental Box**.

Elder Moss says the old records mention it only in fragments: *"a vessel of power, sealed by those who came before."* The true secret of the Elemental Box is that it can be used to upgrade the life suits, granting people powerful elemental abilities. Elder Thorn warns that the forest is crawling with machines, but the council has made their decision. Someone must go.

That someone is you.

## The Journey

Your quest is simple in words, brutal in execution: fight through the eastern forest, past the scrap drones and rust bots, across the river guarded by a heavy mech, and into the far reaches where Vanguard Bots patrol. Somewhere beyond all that rust and ruin lies the Elemental Box.

## The Climax and Trilogy Arc

When you return with the Elemental Box and confront the elders in the Blue House, they attack. Minslaire must fight the corrupted elders for the box. In the end of the first game, they use it to lay waste to village after village and blast you into a remote village set in a rugged mountainous area. Before the end, you grab the Elemental Box and blow it up, releasing raw elemental energy that gives everyone powers. You awaken with the powers of all the elements.

### Part 2: The Sequel
You wake up 5 years later. A powerful figure named **Neegabla** has overthrew the elders, becoming the only other person wielding all elemental powers. Neegabla is revealed to be your younger brother **Tinslaire**, now hardened and nihilistic. After a fierce battle at the end of the second game, you defeat him, reconcile as friends, and swear together to find the true purpose of your lives.

### Part 3: The Trilogy Conclusion
In the third game, your journey for meaning and purpose ultimately leads you to find Islam.
