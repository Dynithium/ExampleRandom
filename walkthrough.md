## Act 1 — The Calling

### Scene 1: The Hum

You wake to the hum.

Dust motes drift in the light from the shuttered window. Two beds, a table, your father's blade in its glass case. The suit is already awake, humming against your skin before you are.

Tinslaire sits on the edge of his bed, wooden bird in his hands. He's been waiting.

*"You're up! Did you hear? The elders called a meeting. They never call meetings."*

He's excited — this is the most interesting thing to happen in weeks. It's morning in the village. Everything is peaceful. The world is as it's always been: fields, dirt paths, the quiet hum, the smell of bread from the Grand Gardens.

Today, the village decides for you — a knock at the door, a voice calling your name. The elders have called a meeting at the Blue House.
